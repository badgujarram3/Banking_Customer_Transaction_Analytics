# Executive Summary

This report presents insights generated from customer, account, transaction, merchant, card, and loan data. The objective is to identify trends, understand customer behavior, evaluate banking products, and support business decision-making.

---

# Project Objectives

- Understand customer demographics
- Analyze account performance
- Evaluate transaction activity
- Study merchant performance
- Analyze loan portfolio
- Review card distribution
- Generate actionable business insights
